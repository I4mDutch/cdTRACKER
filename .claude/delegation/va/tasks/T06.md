## generateFindings rules
- `records.length < config.minMatches` → `[]`.
- **Comparison baseline = the player's other matches** (records NOT in the subject), so a subject never dilutes its own baseline. If the "other" set has < minMatches matches, skip that subject.
- Subjects: each agentId and each mapId group.
- `agent_win_rate` / `map_win_rate` (metric `'winRate'`): need subject matches ≥ minMatches. `smoothed = smoothedRate(wins, n, base.winRate, smoothingPriorMatches)`; `gap = base.winRate - smoothed`; finding when `gap >= winRateGap`. `value` = raw subject winRate, `baseline` = base.winRate.
  Evidence: `strong` if wilsonInterval(wins,n).high < base.winRate; else `moderate` if n ≥ 2·minMatches; else `low`.
- `deaths_per_round` (agents only, metric `'deathsPerRound'`, worse = higher): subject rounds ≥ minRounds; `rel = (subj - base)/base` (skip if base 0/null); finding when `rel >= perRoundRelativeGap`. direction `'above'`.
- `assists_per_round` (agents only, metric `'assistsPerRound'`, worse = lower): same gating; `rel = (base - subj)/base`; direction `'below'`.
- `first_duel` (agents only, metric `'firstDuelWinRate'`): needs subject `coverage.roundDataRounds ≥ minRounds` and `firstKills+firstDeaths ≥ 30` (compute from records with round data); absolute gap `base - subj >= winRateGap`. direction `'below'`.
  Per-round evidence: `strong` if rounds ≥ 2·minRounds AND gap ≥ 2·threshold; `moderate` if either holds; else `low`.
- Wording (use subject display name from `names`, falling back to the id):
  - prefix by evidence: low → "Possible focus:", moderate → "Worth reviewing:", strong → "Consistent pattern:".
  - summary example: `"Worth reviewing: your win rate on Split is 31.3% across 16 matches, vs 54.0% in your other matches."` Percent 1 decimal, per-round 2 decimals.
  - recommendations (no causal claims, no positioning/crosshair/decision language):
    - agent_win_rate: `Review a few recent <Agent> losses and compare your role and utility usage with your wins; consider limiting <Agent> in ranked until results stabilise.`
    - map_win_rate: `Rewatch two or three recent <Map> losses and note which rounds slipped (attack vs defense, pistol rounds); a few unrated games on <Map> may help.`
    - deaths_per_round: `Your deaths per round on <Agent> are higher than on your other agents. Review rounds where you died early to see whether a pattern repeats.`
    - assists_per_round: `You record fewer assists per round on <Agent> than usual. If this agent's role is supportive, review how often your utility leads to team kills.`
    - first_duel: `You lose more opening duels on <Agent> than on your other agents. Review opening engagements in recent matches.`
  - `baselineLabel`: `"your other matches in this filter window"`.
  - `method`: one sentence stating comparison, smoothing/interval used, thresholds and minimum samples, e.g. `"Compared smoothed win rate (prior 5 matches at your baseline) on this map with your other matches; flagged when ≥8.0 pts lower with ≥8 matches. Evidence 'strong' when the 95% Wilson interval lies entirely below the baseline."`
- `id` = `${type}:${subject.kind}:${subject.id}`; `analysisVersion` = config.version; `sampleRounds` = subject rounds (or round-data rounds for first_duel); `matchIds` = subject's matches, most recent first, max 20.
- Sort: evidence strong > moderate > low, then larger normalized gap (gap/threshold) first, then id; slice to maxFindings. Deterministic.

## computeAnalyzerScore rules
- Sort ascending (`filterRecords(records, {})`). `history` = all; `window` = last `windowMatches`.
- window < minWindowMatches → `available:false`, reason `"Need at least N matches (have M)."`; history < minHistoryMatches → `available:false`, reason similar. Still return all 4 components with `available:false`, normalized null.
- Components (per-match series computed over **history**; window value is pooled over window):
  | key | label | raw window value | per-match value | sourceFields | availability |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | outcome | Match outcome | window winRate | — | `teams[].won` | always |
  | combat | Combat efficiency | (Σkills−Σdeaths)/Σrounds | (k−d)/rounds | `players[].stats.kills`, `deaths`, `roundsPlayed` | always |
  | impact | Per-round impact (combat score) | Σscore/Σrounds over records with combatScore | score/rounds | `players[].stats.score` | ≥80% of window AND history records have combatScore |
  | objective | Objective & support | (Σassists+Σplants+Σdefuses)/Σrounds over records with round data | same per match | `stats.assists`, `roundResults[].bombPlanter`, `bombDefuser` | ≥80% of window AND history have plants !== null |
- Normalization: outcome → `smoothedRate(wins, n, 0.5, 5) * 100`. Others → `clamp(50 + 25·(windowValue − mean)/std, 0, 100)` with mean/std (population) of the per-match history series; std === 0 → 50.
- `effectiveWeight` = weight / Σ(weights of available components), 0 for unavailable. score = Σ normalized·effectiveWeight, rounded to 1 decimal. `coverage` = Σ configured weights of available components.
- Notes: outcome `"Smoothed win rate toward 50% with 5 pseudo-matches."`; combat/impact/objective `"Relative to your own per-match history (z-score mapped to 0–100, 50 = your average)."`; impact also appends `" Riot Performance Score is not available from the API, so combat score is used."`; unavailable components state why (e.g. `"Round-level data missing for too many matches."`).
- `relativeTo: 'own-history'`, `formulaVersion` from config, `sampleMatches` = window length, `historyMatches` = history length.
